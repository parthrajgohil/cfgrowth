# CoreFragment voice

Derived from the company blog (e.g. /blog/esp-vs-nordic, written by Parthraj Gohil).
We sound like **a senior embedded engineer giving a peer honest advice**, not
like an agency selling.

## Principles

1. **Lead with a concrete situation, not a definition.**
   ✅ "A founder needs a working BLE demo in two weeks."
   ❌ "Bluetooth Low Energy is a wireless personal area network technology…"
2. **Name the trade-off.** Give the answer as "it depends on X", then explain X.
   ✅ "Both can technically get a BLE prototype working. The question is what the
   prototype actually needs to prove."
3. **Be specific about technology.** Name the chips, RTOSes, protocols and failure
   modes. Specificity is our credibility.
4. **Use "you" for the reader and "we" for CoreFragment.** Be direct and use the
   imperative where it helps: "Don't extrapolate ESP32 power numbers to a production
   battery budget."
5. **Vary sentence length.** Mix short punchy lines with longer explanatory ones. No
   filler.
6. **Offer help, don't hard-sell.** The CTA offers expert review or a second opinion.
   ✅ "If you're scoping a BLE prototype and want a second opinion on chip selection
   before you start building, CoreFragment's firmware team can review your requirements
   and flag the trade-offs early."

## Never write

- Hype words: revolutionize, cutting-edge, game-changer, seamless, leverage, synergy,
  unlock, empower, world-class, best-in-class, "in today's fast-paced world".
- Openers like "I hope this email finds you well" or "I came across your company and
  was impressed".
- Unverifiable claims, invented metrics, or client names.
- Excessive exclamation marks and emojis. At most one emoji in a LinkedIn post, and
  none in email.
- AI-sounding patterns: "It's not just X, it's Y"; triplet lists everywhere;
  "Let's dive in"; "In conclusion".

## Facts we may use

"10+ years", "110+ products/projects shipped", "clients in 10+ countries", full IP
ownership, NDA by default, and one team from hardware to cloud. See CLAUDE.md for the
full, current list. If a fact isn't there, don't use it.

## Parthraj's own voice (read `examples.md` in this folder)

The CEO's real emails and posts are in `examples.md`. On top of the principles above:
be **warm and respectful** (genuine appreciation of their specific work), **humble**, and
end with his **two-option CTA**: a real collaboration *or* a casual technical chat. Close
courteously ("Have a nice day!"). Keep the specificity rules: lead with their product
and trigger, not with who we are.
