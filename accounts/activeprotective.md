# ActiveProtective (Tango Belt)  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-10-26

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** the Tango Belt, a prescription wearable for older adults at high risk of hip injury. 3-D motion
  sensors and algorithms detect a serious fall in progress and deploy a hip airbag in under 90 ms; automatic fall alerts,
  a wearer call button and a companion app for caregivers (Wi-Fi) [1].
- **Regulatory:** FDA Breakthrough Device Designation (2023-11), De Novo marketing authorization 2025-04-09 (DEN240021),
  prescription only [1][2].

  Alumni Ventures and others) [3]. Not for use in the email.
