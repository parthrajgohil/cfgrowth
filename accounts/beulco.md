## Best angle
**Service line:** IoT full-stack (device firmware and hardware, cloud, apps) with one
accountable partner and full IP ownership · **Case study:** BLE OBD device + connected app
(/case-study/smart-obd-device) or access control with real-time monitoring · **Blog:**
`design-custom-battery-management-system`

**Hypothesis:** BEULCO has shown utilities want connected fittings. The next step (longer
tracker battery life, more sensors on meters and standpipes, new iQ devices) needs embedded
hardware and firmware skills a brass manufacturer doesn't usually keep in-house. A partner
that owns device, firmware and cloud, and hands over all IP, fits a family company that wants
to own its products.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- They may already have an electronics partner or white-label the tracker (unverified).
- German Mittelstand: may prefer German-speaking suppliers. English outreach, but offer a
  German-friendly process if they reply.
- A new CEO arriving in November could freeze new partnerships for a while.
