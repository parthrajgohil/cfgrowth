# Bioliberty  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-09-29

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** soft-robotic wearable rehab devices plus software for post-acute care.
  **Lifeglov** is a soft robotic glove that helps both closing and opening hand strength after
  stroke or injury [2]. **Lifehub Clinic** combines AI and robotics to track functional
  recovery in clinics; **Lifehub Home** extends therapy and progress tracking into patients'
  homes [1]. Lifehub and Lifeglov are FDA-registered; eight patents [3].
- **HQ / region:** Edinburgh, UK, with a US base in Boston, MA [1]. Spun out of the University
  of Edinburgh [3].
- **Size:** unverified. Apollo indexes 25 people (firmware, hardware, electronics, Unity,
  Android, clinical).
- **Stage / funding:** $10.2M total in March 2026: $8.2M Series A led by the Scottish National
  Investment Bank ($4M), with Archangels, Eos Advisory, Old College Capital, Hanna Capital SEZC
  and Conduit Connect, plus a $2M pre-Series A [1]. $17.5M raised since the 2023 seed [1].
- **Website:** https://www.bioliberty.com

## Why now (triggers)
- $10.2M raise (2026-03-18) to commercialise **Lifehub Home** and add computer vision to
  Lifehub Clinic, with development and manufacturing in Scotland [1][3]. This is just over 6
  months old, so graded **B**.

## Engineering signals
- Moving a clinic device into homes changes the engineering: unattended use, home WiFi or
  BLE-to-phone links, remote firmware updates, and data that must sync reliably (inference).
- Computer vision is being added to the clinic platform [3]: an AI/ML angle for us.
- Small in-house team with a Head of Firmware, a Hardware Lead, one firmware engineer and an
  electronics engineer (Apollo), so capacity is thin.

## Best angle
**Service line:** firmware + BLE companion apps for a home device (secondary: computer vision
for Lifehub Clinic) · **Case study:** connected health monitoring hub + app
(/case-study/health-monitoring-hub); second: BLE smart glucometer · **Blog:**
`medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`

**Hypothesis:** Lifehub Home means the glove has to work in a patient's living room with no
therapist around: pairing, OTA updates, and data sync over unreliable home networks. A
small firmware team building that while supporting US clinics could use a partner for the
device-to-app-to-cloud path.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- Trigger is slightly older than 6 months (hence B).
- They manufacture in Scotland and value local development [1]; an offshore partner may be a
  harder sell.
- Radio, MCU and app stack are unverified. Don't name them in outreach.
