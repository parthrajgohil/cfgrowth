# Blue Arbor Technologies  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-11-03

## Snapshot
RESTORE Neuromuscular Interface System: electrodes in regenerative muscle grafts on a residual nerve
pick up motor commands; a small wearable wireless External Sensing Unit (ESU) on fine percutaneous
wires sends them to a receiver in the prosthetic hand, for simultaneous finger/wrist/elbow control. A
fully implantable Internal Sensing Unit (ISU) is next · Grass Lake, Michigan, USA (University of
Michigan spin-out) · small team (4 profiles in Apollo) · Series A USD 10.5M (2026-10) · https://bluearbortech.com
