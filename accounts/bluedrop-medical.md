## Best angle
**Service line:** embedded firmware + electronics (primary row: healthcare, Europe) · **Case study:** BLE smart
glucometer (US healthcare org) · **Blog:** `medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`

**Hypothesis:** a home device used daily, with images, temperature and cloud alerts, carries a long tail of firmware,
connectivity, test and manufacturing work as it moves from studies into wider use (and possibly a new scanner
generation). A partner can take defined blocks (a board revision, connectivity/OTA, test fixtures, companion app) while
their team keeps the imaging and clinical side.
