# Cebreo Medical  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-09-30

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** **NeuroBuds®**, a discreet in-ear, medical-grade EEG wearable that looks
  like a hearing aid. Patients apply it themselves for multi-day and multi-night recordings at
  home, with monitoring periods up to three months [1]. Individualised fit, integrated activity
  monitoring, and an AI study/analytics platform with a study-management portal, real-time data
  quality and adherence monitoring [2]. Target uses: epilepsy (seizure counting in drug-resistant
  epilepsy), sleep medicine, neurology research [1][2].
