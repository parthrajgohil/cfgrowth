# Cifarelli S.p.A.  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-11-16

## Snapshot
Professional shoulder-carried and hand-held machines for agriculture and gardening: mist blowers/sprayers, olive and
hook shakers, blowers, vacuums, brushcutters; ~40,000 machines a year, 93% exported (2023) · Voghera (PV), Italy ·
53 employees, revenue EUR 10.7M (2024) · family company, founded 1967 · https://www.cifarelli.it

## Best angle
Service line: IoT full-stack (BLE firmware + companion app + maintenance/data cloud) with embedded hardware underneath
(motor controller, battery/BMS) — segment 2, agriculture (industrial IoT, broad) · Case study: BLE OBD device +
connected app (automotive) · Blog: `design-custom-battery-management-system`.
Hypothesis: a mechanical team that just built its own axial-flux motor now needs the electronics and software around it:
the BLE link on the control unit, the phone app for operators and dealers (runtime, faults, service hours), and battery
management as more models go electric. We can deliver the whole connected layer as one accountable partner, under NDA,
with full IP and source handed over.
