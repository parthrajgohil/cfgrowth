# Coroflo  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-11-02

## Snapshot
Coro: a breastfeeding monitor worn like a normal nipple shield. A patented micro-flow meter in a
slim silicone shield measures flow rate, feed volume and milk temperature and streams them to a
free smartphone app over Bluetooth; the shield charges in a case with contact pins · Dublin,
Ireland (DCU Alpha) · 11 people listed in Apollo (unverified) · seed-stage, Enterprise Ireland and
EU EIC backed · https://www.coroflo.com
