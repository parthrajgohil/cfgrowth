# Daze (DazeTechnology)  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-10-02

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** EV chargers designed and made in Italy: DazePlug (autonomous conductive
  charging), Dazebox Home, Dazebox Share (business, remote management), Dazebox C, and since
  2026-09-14 **Dazebox One**, an affordable home wallbox [1][2][3].
- **HQ / region:** Almenno San Bartolomeo (Bergamo), Lombardy, Italy [4].
- **Size:** 112 employees (2026-05-31) [5]; other sources 83–109.
- **Stage / funding:** founded 2016 by Giacomo Zenoni and Andrea Daminelli; €15M Series A
  (2024-02-13) led by CDP Venture Capital with the EIC Fund, SIMEST, Founders Future, Prana
  Ventures and 035 Investimenti, to grow in Spain, France and new European markets [6].
- **Website:** https://www.daze.eu (also https://www.dazetechnology.com)

## Why now (triggers)
- **New product:** Dazebox One, 2026-09-14 [2]: a cost-focused wallbox with full connectivity
  and multi-charger load balancing, added to an already broad range.
- Series A plan (2024) to expand across Europe [6]: more markets means more grid-code, metering
  and OCPP back-end variants (inference).

## Best angle
**Service line:** embedded firmware + hardware (OCPP, Modbus, BLE/Wi-Fi, BOM optimization,
power design) · **Case study:** none in EV charging; lead with the blog · **Blog:**
`ocpp-compliance-importance`

**Hypothesis:** a 100-person company now carries five charger lines plus a new low-cost one.
Extra firmware hands for OCPP/load-balancing features, or a BOM review on Dazebox One, would let
the core team focus on DazePlug. NDA, full IP transfer.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- EV charging is listed as a website industry and fits "energy" under broad Industrial IoT; it
  is not healthcare.
- They have their own hardware team; the pitch is capacity or cost-down.
