## Best angle
**Service line:** embedded hardware + firmware, with cloud/app for NjordLink+ (connected equipment, explored as industrial
IoT broadly; secondary row) · **Case study:** BLE OBD device + connected app (automotive) · **Blog:** `handle-mqtt-connection-loss`
(boats lose connectivity at sea)

**Hypothesis:** a small UK team ships many new devices a year and is now adding professional ECS hardware and a cloud
platform. Extra firmware/PCB capacity, or taking the NjordLink+ cloud and app side, fits. The CTO is a hands-on hardware
designer, so cloud/app or sustaining work may be the softer entry.
