## Best angle
**Service line:** mobile / cloud apps (secondary row: healthcare), with firmware underneath · **Case study:** BLE smart
glucometer (firmware + Android/iOS apps) · **Blog:** `iomt-development-services-cost-breakdown`

**Hypothesis:** moving Hepatoscope from laptops and tablets to smartphones means driving a USB-C probe from iOS and
Android: USB host support, power budgets, performance on phone hardware, and two native apps that must follow every
regulated software release. A small team focused on ultrasound science may prefer a partner for the mobile layer (and
FHIR/cloud plumbing) with full IP transfer.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- Mostly software; they may build the mobile app in-house. Smartphone plan is undated.
- Funding status unclear (a EUR 10M raise was being prepared at some point; not verified).
- France (EU): opt-out line included.
