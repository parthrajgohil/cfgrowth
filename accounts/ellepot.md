# Ellepot A/S  ·  Fit: A (segment 2)  ·  Researched: 2026-10-05

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** Ellepot paper propagation pots and the fully automatic machines that make
  them (FlexAir 2.0, Jumbo Flexline, Multiflex), sold to growers and forestry nurseries in 130+
  countries [1][2]. Connected layer: **Ellepot Live**, a production-management app (iOS/Android)
  and web portal showing machine and production status, preventive-maintenance warnings,
  troubleshooting alarms and spare-part / paper ordering [3]. Some machines also exchange orders
  and production data with customers' ERP via "two-way communicative devices" (Industry 4.0) [4].
- **HQ / region:** Storstrømsvej 55, Esbjerg, Denmark [2].
- **Size:** ~75 employees in Esbjerg (via search summary) [5]; Apollo lists 47 people [9].
- **Stage / funding:** founded 1992 (via search summary) [5]; privately held, chairman Carsten
  Bjerg [5]. No outside funding found.
- **Website:** https://www.ellepot.com

## Best angle
**Service line:** IoT full-stack with hardware/firmware underneath (machine-side gateway or sensor
board, firmware, MQTT/cloud link, app features) · **Case study:** CNC drilling machine automation
for a European industrial automation company
(/case-study/industrial-cnc-drilling-machine-automation) · **Blog:** `handle-mqtt-connection-loss`

**Hypothesis:** Ellepot now ships every new machine connected and wants Ellepot Live on the
installed base. A small retrofit kit for older machines, or new sensing (substrate, cutter wear),
needs electronics and firmware work that a ~75-person machine maker may not staff in-house. NDA,
full IP transfer.
