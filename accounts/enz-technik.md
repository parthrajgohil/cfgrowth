# enz technik AG  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-10-09

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** water-jetting nozzles and cleaning tools for sewer, pipe and industrial cleaning (up to 3,000 bar),
  and a connected line: the **enz camera nozzle** (eBomb, eBulldog) with WiFi, app, **MyEnz Cloud** and Google Maps
  mapping of manholes, a camera firmware/app update in 2025-02, the **MyEnz App** (2025-04), and the **JetCam 20**
  inspection system (2025-07) [1][2][3]. The company brands itself "your (camera) nozzle specialist" [5].
- **Segment 2:** a traditional mechanical manufacturer with a connected product. The camera nozzle's electronics,
  Linux firmware, iOS/Android/web app, AWS cloud and wireless charging were developed by the design house Helbling,
  not in-house [4]. Apollo shows mechanical and prototyping engineers but no electronics or firmware engineers [5].
- **HQ / region:** Giswil, Switzerland; subsidiaries in Germany, Sweden, UK and USA; ~200 distribution partners in 50+
  countries [1].
- **Size:** unverified (~31 people in Apollo [5]).
- **Funding:** private (family-run per its own claims; unverified).

## Best angle
**Service line:** IoT full-stack (device electronics + firmware + app + cloud) for a company without an electronics team ·
**Case study:** BLE OBD device + connected app (/case-study/smart-obd-device) · **Blog:** `handle-mqtt-connection-loss`

**Hypothesis:** enz already proved a connected nozzle sells. The next generation (longer runtime, better video, sensing
of deposits or position, cheaper BOM) needs electronics, firmware, app and cloud work they don't have in-house. We offer
one accountable partner for the whole stack, full IP and source transfer, so the knowledge stays at enz.
