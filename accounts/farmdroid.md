# FarmDroid  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-11-04

## Snapshot
Solar-powered autonomous field robots that seed and mechanically weed row crops without herbicides
(FD20; new FD60 launched 2026-09-01), navigating on 8 mm RTK GPS with no cameras · Vejen, Denmark ·
~33–41 people (Danish registry data, see below) · ~EUR 10.5M round (2024-10) · https://farmdroid.com

## Best angle
Service line: hardware / power design + embedded firmware (primary row: industrial IoT, read broadly to include
agriculture) · Case study: CNC drilling machine automation for a European industrial automation company
(/case-study/industrial-cnc-drilling-machine-automation) · Blog: `design-custom-battery-management-system`.
FarmDroid is taking the FD60 from launch to first deliveries in January 2027 with a small team. The new solar +
battery + range-extender power system, field connectivity and OTA, and BOM cost on a second platform are where
outside firmware/hardware capacity (and re-engineering / cost-down on the FD20) can help without new hires.
