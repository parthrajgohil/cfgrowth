# German Bionic  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-09-30

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** connected, AI-assisted powered exoskeletons for physically demanding work.
  **Exia** (unveiled at CES 2026): up to 38 kg of dynamic lift support per movement, supports
  walking, carrying and bent-over work, cloud-connected with over-the-air updates, German Bionic
  Connect app (iOS/Android) and the German Bionic IO data platform for fleet management [1][2].
  Also Apogee and Cray X [2]. Markets: logistics, manufacturing, construction, baggage handling,
  nursing and elderly care [2].
- **HQ / region:** Augsburg, Germany (per Revelio/Tracxn summaries); offices in Berlin, Boston
  and Tokyo [3][4]. The CES release gives Berlin [1].
- **Size:** 94 employees (March 2026), down ~10% year on year [3].
- **Stage / funding:** $39.8M over 4 rounds; latest $16.3M on 2023-12-11; investors include
  Samsung Catalyst Fund, Storm Ventures, MIG; Mubea is the industrialisation partner [4].
- **Website:** https://www.germanbionic.com
