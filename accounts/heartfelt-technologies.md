# Heartfelt Technologies  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-10-29

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** an automatic, AI-supported, non-contact telemonitor for heart-failure patients: a wall-mounted
  optical sensor (about the size of a smart speaker, usually by the bed) measures foot and lower-leg volume whenever the
  patient walks near it; data goes to the cloud and changes trigger alerts for carers and clinicians [1][2]. It takes
  1,800 images a minute and uses AI to detect the foot's position [2]. "Not available for sale at the moment"; provided
  through clinical teams [1].
- **HQ / region:** Cambridge, UK (St John's Innovation Park); offices in Cork, Ireland and Woburn MA, US [1].
- **Size:** ~27 employees (Caplight, 2025-08, third-party) [3]; Apollo lists 22 people records [5].
- **Stage / funding:** angel and VC rounds (Cambridge Angels, o2h ventures, Cambridge Capital Group) and Innovate UK
  grants per Caplight [3]; amounts unverified.
- **Website:** https://www.hftech.org
