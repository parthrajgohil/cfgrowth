## Best angle
Service line: embedded firmware and hardware (primary, healthcare) · Case study: BLE smart
glucometer (/case-study/smart-glucometer) · Blog: `medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`.
A cellular, single-patch, overnight device lives or dies on modem power, upload retries and
battery margin. With SANSA GO putting patches in clinic staff's hands nationwide and new metrics
being added, the next hardware or firmware revision is likely on the roadmap. We can add
firmware and hardware capacity (low-power, cellular, IEC 62304-aware) without them hiring.
