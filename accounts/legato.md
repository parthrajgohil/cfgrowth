## Best angle
**Service line:** Mobile apps (BLE companion app) + embedded firmware (low-power, OTA) ·
**Case study:** BLE smart glucometer, firmware + Android/iOS apps for a US healthcare org
(/case-study/smart-glucometer) · **Blog:** `medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`

**Hypothesis:** the acoustic and DSP core is their IP, and they won't outsource it. But a
fall launch of an OTC hearing product still needs a solid companion app (fitting and
personalization, pairing, OTA), field-proof OTA and battery-life tuning. Those jobs often
slip at a small team focused on the acoustics. We can take the app and OTA/BLE
work under NDA with full IP transfer.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- A very experienced consumer-wearables founding team (Bose Frames, Meta Ray-Bans) that
  probably already has launch partners. Outreach must be specific and modest.
- It's unverified whether the product has a companion app or BLE. The angle is a hypothesis;
  ask, don't assert.
- OTC hearing aids are FDA-regulated. CLAUDE.md lists no FDA/hearing-aid experience, so don't claim any.
- It's this late in the launch cycle, so the timing might favour post-launch work (v2, cost-down, re-engineering).
