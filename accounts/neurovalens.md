## Best angle
**Service line:** embedded firmware + hardware for medical wearables (primary row: healthcare firmware/hardware), with BLE
companion-app experience · **Case study:** BLE smart glucometer (firmware + Android/iOS apps for a US healthcare org) ·
**Blog:** `medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`

**Hypothesis:** Neurovalens now runs four cleared indications on one headset and is scaling US volume through the VA. Its
visible team is clinical, regulatory, QA and app engineers, with no firmware or electronics staff. A partner who can take on
firmware and hardware work (next-gen headset, cost-down for volume, power/BLE robustness, production test) gives the Chief
Project Officer capacity without hiring, with IP and design files handed over.
