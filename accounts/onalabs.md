# Onalabs Inno-Hub  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-10-08

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** non-invasive wearables that read biomarkers in sweat with patented electrochemical sensors and
  disposable cartridges, connected to a mobile app and cloud platform [1][2]. **ONASPORT** (chest strap: lactate,
  electrolyte loss, dehydration, sodium, heart rate) is on sale; **ONAVITAL** (blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen
  saturation, temperature) is in clinical validation; a diabetes-focused line is in development [1].
- **HQ / region:** Barcelona, Spain [1].
- **Size:** ~30 people [1].
- **Funding:** EUR 9.3M round announced 2026-07-27, led by SETT (EUR 4.6M, NextGenerationEU), with 1921 Ventures,
  nPrime, Reig Patrimonia and WA4STEAM [1]. Total raised unverified.
- **Quality:** ISO 13485; manufacturing licence from the Spanish medicines agency (AEMPS) [2].
- **Website:** https://www.onalabs.com
