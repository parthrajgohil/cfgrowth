# Plasmacure  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-10-19

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** PLASOMA, a cold-plasma wound therapy for complex wounds (e.g. diabetic foot ulcers). A portable "pulser"
  sends controlled electrical pulses to a single-use pad, generating cold plasma from air in the wound bed ("Dual Plasma":
  cold plasma plus mild electrostimulation, ~2-minute treatment) [1][2].
- **HQ / region:** Eindhoven and Nijmegen, Netherlands [2]
- **Size:** 14 people on the team page (two R&D engineers) [3]
- **Funding:** EUR 6M Series A (2025-07) led by Venture Medical (US), which also committed over USD 10M for US regulatory,
  reimbursement and market entry and becomes the US distributor; VGZ, Noaber, EIC Fund, Oost NL also invested [2][4]. Not for use in outreach.
- **Website:** https://www.plasoma.com (plasmacure.nl redirects)
