# REEV  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-11-03

## Snapshot
Wearable robotics for neurological gait disorders: DREEVEN, an electro-hydraulic motorized knee
orthosis that turns a passive knee-ankle-foot orthosis (KAFO) into an assisted one, driven by gait
data from REEV SENSE (two shoe/leg-worn sensors with a clinician app) · Toulouse, France and Boston,
USA · ~30 people (company release; 25 per FrenchWeb) · Series A USD 9.2M (2025-02) · https://www.reev.care
