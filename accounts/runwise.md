## Best angle
**Service line:** hardware / PCB (low-power wireless sensors, BOM optimization, power design) plus
firmware · **Case study:** access control with real-time monitoring (/case-study/access-control-system) ·
**Blog:** `handle-mqtt-connection-loss`

**Hypothesis:** Runwise is widening from heating into water with a new wireless sensor and a Gen2
platform. A second hardware team for the sensor line, or a BOM / battery-life cost-down pass,
would speed up the roadmap. Under NDA, full IP transfer.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- They design their own hardware already; the pitch is capacity or cost-down.
- Runwise positions itself on software and data revenue; hardware budget may be limited.
- Segment note: a technology company, not a traditional one; counted as Industrial IoT (facilities).
