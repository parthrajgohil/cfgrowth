# Tended  ·  Fit: A  ·  Researched: 2026-10-05

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** wearable geofencing safety devices for trackside rail and construction
  workers. A positioning device worn on PPE alerts the wearer (audible, visual, vibration) in
  under 0.25 s when they leave a safe zone; machinery-mounted trackers, geotagging and **Virtual
  Worksite Marker Boards** give supervisors real-time positions [1][2][3]. Earlier product: Tended
  Protect fall-detection wristband [4].
- **HQ / region:** Lincoln, UK [5].
- **Size:** ~30 employees (Dealroom/Bouncewatch via search summary) [5]; Apollo lists 28 people,
  including a Head of Hardware Engineering, a Head of Engineering and 2 embedded engineers [9].
- **Stage / funding:** £2M (May 2025, Praetura Ventures + Blackfinch Ventures) to grow sales and
  engineering, strengthen IP and **develop next-generation wearable safety devices** [2]; £2.4M
  in 2021 [4]. Total raised unverified.
