## Best angle
**Service line:** embedded hardware + firmware for medical devices (sensor boards, motor/power
electronics, BLE, HIL testing, re-engineering/cost-down of older boards) · **Case study:**
connected health monitoring hub + app (/case-study/health-monitoring-hub) · **Blog:**
`medical-wearable-device-development-challenges-and-fix`

**Hypothesis:** each new device (Elvis now) adds electronics to design and keep certified while
older devices need upkeep and end-of-life part swaps. An outside team for a board redesign or
the next device lets the in-house electronics team stay on the core line. NDA, full IP transfer.
