# UNTHA shredding technology  ·  Fit: A (segment 2)  ·  Researched: 2026-10-02

## Snapshot
- **What they make:** industrial shredders (1-, 2- and 4-shaft; mobile XR and ZR mobil-e;
  secondary SRF shredders) for recycling, waste-to-energy and production waste. 10,000+ machines
  installed worldwide [1]. Connected layer: **UNTHA GENIUS** condition monitoring (100+ operating
  channels per machine: power, temperatures, vibration, EN 50160 power quality) shown in the
  **MyUNTHA** customer portal, with predictive-maintenance alerts [2][3].
- **HQ / region:** Kuchl (Salzburg), Austria [1].
- **Size:** ~190 at the Kuchl site; 201–500 total (sources differ) [1].
- **Stage / funding:** founded 1970 by Anton Unterwurzacher; family-founded, privately held. No
  outside funding found.
- **Website:** https://www.untha.com

## Best angle
**Service line:** IoT full-stack with hardware/firmware underneath (sensor and edge boards,
gateway firmware, MQTT/cloud link, machine-to-machine interfaces) · **Case study:** CNC drilling
machine automation for a European industrial automation company
(/case-study/industrial-cnc-drilling-machine-automation) · **Blog:** `handle-mqtt-connection-loss`

**Hypothesis:** UNTHA's smart-products work spans GENIUS on every new machine, a new partner
interface and a portal. One partner that can build edge electronics, firmware and the data link
would speed up the next interfaces (other material handlers, conveyors) without growing a full
electronics team. NDA, full IP transfer.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- They already use A1 Digital for connectivity and platform; our scope is the on-machine
  electronics and firmware, not replacing A1.
- Electrical engineering may be mostly PLC/industrial controls; custom electronics appetite
  unverified.
