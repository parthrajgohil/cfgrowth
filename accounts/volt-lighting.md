## Snapshot

  transformers), sold direct to consumers and contractors; also VOLT Grow (horticulture), pool &
  spa, and AMP Lighting (contractor brand) [1][2]. Connected line: **VOLT Smart**: Smart LED

## Best angle
**Service line:** IoT full-stack (BLE mesh firmware, gateway, iOS/Android app), with hardware
underneath · **Case study:** BLE OBD device + connected app (/case-study/smart-obd-device) ·
**Blog:** `design-ble-devices-for-crowded-rf-environments`

**Hypothesis:** a lighting manufacturer with a growing smart line and a new leadership team may
want one accountable partner for the firmware, gateway and app as the range expands. Full IP
ownership matters to a company whose core is fixtures, not electronics.
