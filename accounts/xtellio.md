# Xtellio (Xtel Wireless ApS)  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-11-13

## Snapshot
Jobsite telematics for rental, construction and utility fleets: 32+ battery-powered Bluetooth Xense sensors for small
assets and non-powered tools, Xentral cellular gateways (LTE-M, NB-IoT, legacy 2G), and wired Pro-Xentral devices
(Standard, CANbus, Heat IQ for industrial heaters), with data delivered through open APIs · Aalborg, Denmark ·
~10 employees (registry listings 7–10) · founded 2005 by engineers (press); legal entity Xtel Wireless ApS, CVR
38226835, registered 2016 · https://www.xtellio.com
