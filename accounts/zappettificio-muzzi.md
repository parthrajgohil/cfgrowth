# Zappettificio Muzzi  ·  Fit: B  ·  Researched: 2026-11-16

## Snapshot
Forged wear parts for soil-working machines (rotary-tiller hoes, ploughshares, power-harrow tines, mulcher knives,
trencher blades, spading-machine spades, custom parts): 4,000–5,000+ models, over 4 million pieces a year, ~75%
exported · Castel Guelfo di Bologna, Italy · 50–99 employees (aziende.it estimate), revenue EUR 18.1M (2024) ·
worker cooperative (soc. coop. a r.l.) founded 1981 · https://www.muzzi.com

## Why now (triggers)
- **Verified, recent:** VigilaSense award 2026-09-29, shown at EIMA 2026-11-10/14. The commercial director says they want
  to "understand better the relationship between tool, soil and working conditions" (Carlino), i.e. data from the field.

  product) is unknown.

## Engineering signals
- Apollo shows a Technical Office Manager, technical office staff, a process engineer, production/purchasing and sales
  directors. **No electronics or firmware engineer**: a forge building its first electronic product.
- Scaling a sensor to "any implement" at their volumes means a rugged, sealed, low-power node, cost-down, ISOBUS
  integration, a phone/cloud path for farmers and dealers, and field updates.

## Best angle
Service line: Embedded hardware + firmware for a rugged field sensor node (ISOBUS, low power), plus app/cloud — segment 2,
agriculture (industrial IoT, broad) · Case study: BLE OBD device + connected app (automotive) · Blog:
`handle-mqtt-connection-loss`.
Hypothesis: B4 owns the algorithm; productising the hardware for thousands of implements is a different job (enclosure
and sealing, battery life, BOM cost, ISOBUS certification, manufacturing test, OTA). We can take that slice, or the app,
under NDA with full IP handed over, so the cooperative owns its product.

## Risks / disqualifiers
- B4 and the University of Messina may cover hardware too; we would be a third party.
- Cooperative governance may slow decisions. Italian-language outreach likely needed.
- Headcount estimate only (50–99).
