## Post

At 9 a.m. in Ahmedabad, it's still last night in Boston.

Since 2015, we've built products with clients in more than 10 countries. People often ask how hardware work survives a 9½-hour time difference. Here is why it's less of a problem than it sounds ->

Firmware builds can be shared in minutes.

Boards can be couriered.

Logs, scope captures and test videos travel overnight.

The hard part is a question that waits a whole day for an answer.

"Which battery did you mean?" sent at 6 p.m. in Ahmedabad lands in Boston at 8:30 a.m. If nobody wrote down the options, the reply comes back the next night, and the project just lost a day.

So we try to end each day with an update that carries an answer, not just a question: what we tested, what we found, and two options with the one we'd recommend.

When you start your morning, it's a decision, not a discussion.

Parthraj explains it in under a minute in the video.

If you work with engineers in another time zone, try it for a week: no question goes out without its options.
