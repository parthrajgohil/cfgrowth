## Post

A good engineering partner should be easy to leave.

That sounds odd from a services company. Here is why we mean it ->

For a company building its first connected product, say a tool maker adding tracking or an equipment maker adding remote monitoring, the biggest worry is rarely the chip.

It's this: "What happens if we stop working with you?"

So we try to answer it before the project starts.

The NDA comes first, by default.

The IP is yours: hardware, firmware, apps and cloud.

At handover you get the complete source code and design files, not only a binary and a PDF.

A simple test helps here: could another competent team pick up those files and build version two without calling us?

If the answer is no, the product isn't fully yours yet.

Parthraj explains it in under a minute in the video.

When you choose a partner for your first device, ask on day one what you'll own on the last day.
