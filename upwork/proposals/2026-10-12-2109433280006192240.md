Hi,

I read your brief for the geophone stake, and it's close to the work I do most: battery-powered sensor devices on STM32 and Nordic, from schematic and layout to low-power firmware and field testing. [YOUR LINE: your closest low-power sensor or LoRa project, and what the precision-ADC side was like]

One thing I'd plan for early: the dipole sits inside a PVC tube, partly in the ground, with the battery pack below it. The soil and the tube will detune it, so I'd tune it in a tube in real soil, not on the bench, and measure range to your mast in the first field week. The hardest wireless bugs I've found showed up in field testing, because everything works in the lab.

MCU: I'd go with the STM32WLE5. One chip for MCU and radio saves space in a 40 mm tube, and the LoRaWAN stack is mature. The nRF52840 + SX1262 only pays off if you want BLE for phone-based installation.

On power, the 15 mW target is mostly decided by the ADS1288 and how often the MCU wakes at 500 SPS, not by the radio. I'd measure that in milestone 1 before sizing the LiFePO4 pack.

4. 10 units and acceptance tests: $2,400 plus parts and assembly at cost, 3 weeks

All IP and source are yours, and an NDA is fine.

One question: how long should a unit run between charges? That sets the pack size and how hard we push below 15 mW.
