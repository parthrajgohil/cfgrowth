Hi,

I read your brief for the Motion-Rest controller. Taking over an existing ESP32-S3 codebase and finishing it, rather than restarting, is work I know well.

Two things I'd check first. If position comes from Hall pulses, count them on the S3's PCNT peripheral, not in GPIO interrupts: pulses missed at direction changes make the count drift over hundreds of cycles, so each routine needs a re-home against a limit switch. And keep the duty-cycle budget (on-time and rest owed) in NVS, so a power cycle can't wipe out a rest period the actuator still needs.
