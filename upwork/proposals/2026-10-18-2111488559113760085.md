## Cover letter
```
Hi,

I read your Phase 1 plan for the wellness device. Timed outputs, audio, USB-C, NFC and a tight battery budget in one small product is close to the work I do most. I've worked with Nordic chips for eight years, and I've built an audio streaming device on the nRF5340.

One thing I'd design in from day one: drive the timed outputs from the audio clock, not from a separate timer. The I2S clock and the system timer come from different sources, so over a long session they drift apart and the switching slides out of step with the sound. If the GPIO/PWM events are counted off the I2S sample stream, they stay locked to the audio for as long as it plays.

Your six points, briefly:
1. BLE glucometer firmware with Android and iOS apps for a US healthcare company, a connected health monitoring hub with its app, and a BLE OBD device for cars. I led the engineering on each, from architecture through firmware and bring-up.
2. nRF5340: low power, I2S, USB and an NFC tag interface on chip, and BLE if you add an app later. I'd only pick an ESP32 if you need Wi-Fi.
3. $40/hr. A works-like prototype on dev boards at this scope usually takes 80 to 120 hours, firmware included. I'll firm that up once I've read the brief.

6. Yes to the NDA and IP assignment. Code, schematics and files go into your GitHub.

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.

Parthraj
```
