## Screening answers
1. *Built a device on Find My or a similar tracker?* Not on Find My itself, to be straight with you.
   I've built BLE devices on Nordic nRF52 for 8 years, including an ongoing nRF52 firmware contract
   since 2022 and a BLE smart glucometer with its iOS and Android apps. For Find My I'd use Nordic's
   Find My add-on for the nRF Connect SDK, which becomes available once your company is enrolled in
   Apple's MFi program.
2. *How to detect card removal slim and low-power?* A low-profile switch under each slot, wired as a
   wake-up interrupt, so it draws nothing until a card moves. A Hall sensor and a small magnet for
   the cash flap. Capacitive or optical sensing is possible but costs standby current, so I'd use it
   only if switches don't fit the thickness. Battery life then mostly comes down to the BLE
   advertising interval, which we'd set together.
3. *Certifications?* No personal certifications for this. What the product will need: FCC (and CE if
   sold in Europe) for the radio, which is simpler with a pre-certified module than a chip-down
   design but costs some thickness, and Apple's Find My certification under your MFi membership.
   I'd plan the board so both stay straightforward.
