The risk I'd test first is iPhone to iPhone with both apps in the background. A backgrounded iOS app's advert loses the local name, and its service UUID moves to Apple's overflow area, with no room for custom data like a rotating ID. Another backgrounded iPhone only finds it by scanning for that exact UUID, and Android can't see it reliably. So the rotating identifier usually has to be read over a short GATT connection, which changes battery use, the matching service and the privacy design. I'd make that the paid prototype test, on real phones in pockets, not on a desk.

The wearable phase helps: a device that advertises removes most of these limits, so I'd design the identifier protocol for the wearable to take over later.

Which iOS and Android versions does the pilot need to support?

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.

Thanks,
Parthraj
