## Cover letter
```
Hi,

A 20 mm device with a global-shutter camera, an IMU and BLE is a hard packaging problem, and I like that you're starting with a feasibility study. I'd do the same. For over 10 years I've built embedded hardware and firmware for clients in 10+ countries, and on a new device I always de-risk the hardest parts first.

The first thing I'd check is the camera interface, because it picks the processor. Many of the smallest global-shutter sensors output MIPI CSI-2, while the low-power BLE chips I'd normally choose for a battery this small (Nordic nRF52/nRF53) have no camera port at all. So it's a sensor with a parallel or SPI output, an MCU with a camera port plus a BLE chip, or a bridge, and each one changes the board stack, the RAM you need to hold a frame, and the power budget. The second check is BLE throughput: an uncompressed frame can take seconds to send, so the study should say how many images per event you can really get off the device.

Closest work: I built a knee-worn wearable that tracks movement, bending and steps with an IMU, and I've worked with Nordic BLE chips for 8 years. My camera work so far has been on larger systems (computer vision for PCB fault detection, Jetson-based video setups), not a camera in 20 mm, which is exactly why the study matters. I'd cover the electronics and the PCB and component stack-up cross-sections, and work with your mechanical designer on sealing and impact.

For the deliverables you listed I'd plan about 30 hours over two weeks, capped so the cost is fixed. Happy to sign your NDA and IP terms first.

What resolution and how many frames per event do you need?

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.

Thanks,
Parthraj
```
