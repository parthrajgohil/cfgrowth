## Cover letter

Hi,

I read your brief for the camera and sensor recorder, and it's my kind of board: a compute-module carrier with an MCU doing the real-time sensing and Linux doing the recording. 10+ years in embedded hardware and firmware, 110+ projects, clients in 10+ countries.

One thing to settle before the schematic: the CM5 has no hardware H.264 encoder (the Pi 5 generation dropped it). In standalone mode, two 1080p30 streams either come from cameras that already output MJPEG or H.264, written to the card as-is, or get encoded in software, which loads the CPU and heats the board. That decides the camera list, the SD write rate and the thermals. Computer mode also needs the hub's upstream switched between the USB-C port and the CM5 (a SuperSpeed 2:1 switch), and a power budget per mode, since a laptop port may not carry two 900 mA cameras.

Closest work: boards for a client's product lines over a five-year hardware contract, and a Raspberry Pi based HMI for a CNC drilling machine. I haven't laid out a CM5 carrier with a USB3 hub before, so I'd follow Raspberry Pi's CM5 design guidelines closely on the USB3 routing.

I'd start with a short audit of your brief and parts, then schematic and layout within the week, then bring-up with power, USB, sensor and recording tests.

Which cameras do you plan to use, and do they output MJPEG?

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.
