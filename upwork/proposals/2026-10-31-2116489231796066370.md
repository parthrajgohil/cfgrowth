## Cover letter
```
Hi,

Your nRF52840 + MAX-10S board is close to the work I know best. Nordic chips have been my main platform for 8 years, and battery-powered BLE devices, board and firmware, are the core of my engineering company's work. I also built a knee-worn rehab wearable that tracks movement and steps, so the IMU and low-power side is familiar.

One thing I'd settle in the schematic milestone: feed the MAX-10S V_BCKP pin from an always-on rail off the battery, not the switched 3.3 V. Without it, every power-up is a cold start, and the receiver spends far more battery searching for satellites than it saves by being off. At 60x30 mm I'd also put the GNSS patch and the module antenna at opposite ends, with the Li-Po kept clear of both.

For the IMU I'd lean towards the BMI270: its any-motion wake lets the nRF52840 and the GPS sleep while the device sits still.

My quote: $900 fixed in your three milestones (schematic $300, layout $450, Gerbers/BOM/CPL $150). Schematic for your review in about 4 working days, final files in about 2 weeks, JLCPCB/LCSC parts where possible, KiCad source and full ownership to you.

One question: does the device need continuous tracking, or a fix every few minutes? That sets the power budget more than any part choice.

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.

Thanks,
Parthraj
```
