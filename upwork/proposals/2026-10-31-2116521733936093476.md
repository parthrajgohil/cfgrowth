Hi,

Turning your working Waveshare ESP32-P4 prototype into a compact production board is the kind of job I do most. Over 10+ years and 110+ projects I've taken many dev-board prototypes to manufactured boards, ESP32 devices among them, including the hardware and firmware for a WLED controller and firmware for an ESP32-C6 relay board here on Upwork.

One thing I'd lock down in the feasibility review: keep the P4-to-C6 link exactly as the Waveshare board wires it, including the C6 reset and boot lines under P4 control.
