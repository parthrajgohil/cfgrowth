## Cover letter
```
Hi,

Your marine HVAC controller is the kind of board pair I enjoy: a real product, firmware already running, and a brief detailed down to the I2C addresses. I've been designing and bringing up ESP32 product hardware for over 10 years, including the hardware and firmware for a WLED controller here on Upwork and five years of embedded hardware for one client's product lines.

The thing I'd watch on the main board is ground. Three motor channels and a 24V compressor relay switch real current, and the ADS1115 next to them reads millivolts. If the motor and relay return currents share copper with the ADC and the I2C bus, your sensor readings will jump every time the compressor kicks in. I'd give the power stage its own corner and its own return path to the input, keep the I2C lines clear of it, and make sure the 24V input has a TVS for the spikes boat DC systems see, if the full brief doesn't already include one.

I work in KiCad and would deliver the BOM and CPL in JLCPCB's format. With your netlists I'd estimate two to three weeks from brief to Gerbers for both boards, at a fixed $2,400 for the full scope including your two review rounds, and $45/hr for extra revisions. Happy to sign your NDA first; all files and IP are yours.

One question: will the main board sit in a sealed box near the compressor or in a dry cabin space? That decides conformal coating and connector choices.

Happy to talk it through on a short call here on Upwork.

Thanks,
Parthraj
```
