## Cover letter
```
Hi,

Your brief describes how I've worked for over 10 years: owning a connected product end to end, from the schematic to the dashboard. My engineering company has delivered 110+ projects for clients in 10+ countries, across PCB design, firmware, cloud and apps.

The work closest to your list: a BLE glucometer where I built the firmware and the Android and iOS apps, and a connected health monitoring hub with its app and cloud back end, so yes, complete solutions from device to app. I've also built the HMI and automation for a European CNC drilling machine maker on Raspberry Pi, and computer vision that finds faults on PCBs.

One thing I raise early when a prototype heads for production is device identity and updates. If every unit doesn't get its own certificate and a tested, rollback-safe OTA path before the first batch ships, you end up re-flashing units by hand in the field. So I design factory provisioning and the bootloader into the first board revision, not the third.

The platforms I use most are ESP32, STM32 and Nordic nRF52 (Nordic for 8 years now), on FreeRTOS, Zephyr or bare metal, plus embedded Linux on Raspberry Pi. On the cloud side, AWS IoT or Azure IoT over MQTT, with React dashboards and InfluxDB for the time series.
```
