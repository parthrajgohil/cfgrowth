## Screening answers
1. Which BLE board and optical pulse sensor would you use and why?
   The XIAO nRF52840 Sense is a good pick: Nordic BLE with low sleep current, an on-board IMU for tap wake and motion, Li-Po charging, and QSPI flash to store sessions. For the sensor I'd use a MAX30102 breakout on the IR channel, which gives a strong signal at the fingertip and can sample well above 100 Hz. I'd skip the MAX86150: its ECG needs two-hand contact, which a 25-30 mm one-thumb device can't use.
2. Have you built anything that measures heart rate or HRV from a PPG signal?
   Not a PPG product, honestly. My closest work is health devices: BLE firmware and Android/iOS apps for a smart glucometer, a connected health monitoring hub, and a knee-worn movement-tracking wearable for physical therapy, all with sensor sampling, sleep modes and BLE data transfer.
3. How would you work out beat-to-beat intervals from the raw signal, and how would you deal with motion?
   Band-pass the raw IR signal (about 0.5-5 Hz), find each beat on the steepest upslope or the peak with an adaptive threshold and a refractory window, and interpolate the peak to get sub-sample timing. For motion, I'd flag any beat where the IMU shows movement or the interval jumps far from the local median, and keep the raw signal in the CSV so flagged beats can be checked against the H10 rather than silently dropped.
4. Can you deliver milestone 1 within two weeks of the parts arriving?
   Yes. Milestone 1 (bench reading, tap start/stop, CSV download) fits comfortably in two weeks from the parts arriving.
